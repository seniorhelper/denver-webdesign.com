The difference nobody explains

A designer builds it. An agency manages building it.

When you hire a Denver agency, a meaningful slice of what you pay covers the machinery of running an agency: the office, the account manager, the project manager, the sales commission, the strategist who writes the deck. Some of that is genuinely valuable on a large project with a dozen stakeholders.

On a website for a Denver business with five services and a service area, most of it is overhead you are funding but not using. You end up paying for four people to coordinate about work that one person could have finished.

The person who understands your business best should be the person writing the code. Every layer between those two things is a place where meaning gets lost.

Do this before you hire anybody

Test your current website. It takes thirty seconds.

Google publishes a free tool called PageSpeed Insights. Paste in any web address and it scores the page out of 100 in four categories. It's the same tool Google uses, it's free, and it doesn't care who built the site.

This is the single most useful thing you can do before spending money on a website designer. Run it on your current site. Then run it on the website of whoever is pitching you.

What the numbers usually mean

If Performance comes back under 50 on mobile, the site is almost certainly a heavy theme with a stack of plugins, oversized images, or both. That's the most common condition of small business websites in Denver, and it's fixable.

If SEO comes back low, the page is missing basic structure — titles, descriptions, headings, a way for crawlers to understand it. That one is usually cheap to fix and worth doing immediately.

If Best Practices is low, there's often an actual security or standards problem sitting there, quietly.

Straight answers

Questions about hiring a website designer in Denver

How much does a website designer cost in Denver CO?

Our custom-coded sites start at $99 per month, and drag-and-drop builder plans start at $5.99 per month. For comparison, Denver agencies commonly quote $9,000 to $22,000 for a professional five-page website, and published guides across the wider market put most professional builds between $3,000 and $15,000. Rates rise with size, complexity and how much optimization is included.

What is the difference between a website designer and a web design company?

Mostly headcount and overhead. A company has a team and a process, with a manager coordinating specialists. A designer does the work directly. The company model earns its cost on large projects with many stakeholders. For a local business with a handful of services, you're usually paying for coordination that the project doesn't need.

Do I need a designer near me, or does remote work fine?

The build works identically either way. Local matters when your customers search with local intent — knowing which Denver suburbs actually matter for your service, what the competition already ranks for, how people here phrase things. If you sell nationally, paying a local premium buys you very little.

How long does it take to design a website?

Some single-page sites go live within 24 hours. A typical multi-page business site takes seven days or more. Ecommerce runs two to four weeks. Apps take 30 to 60 days and up. The thing that moves the timeline most is how fast decisions get made on your side, not how fast the code gets written.

What do you need from me before we start?

Fifteen minutes on the phone, and whatever you already have — logo, photos, an old website, a brochure. If you don't have written content we'll write it from the call and you correct it. Waiting for the business owner to produce copy is what kills most website projects, so we don't put that on you.

Will my website work on phones?

Every site is designed for phones first. Most local searches happen on a phone, often on a patchy connection. If the page is slow to load or hard to tap, none of the rest of the work matters. You can check any site yourself with Google's free PageSpeed Insights tool.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting over?

Often that's the better move. If the site already ranks for anything, throwing it out means throwing that away too. We'd rather keep what works and fix what doesn't. If it's built on something unmaintainable or fundamentally slow, a rebuild usually costs less over two years than endless patching.

Do I own my website and domain?

Your domain name is registered to you and stays yours. Your content, photos and logo are yours. On a monthly plan the site itself is leased with a buyout option at 24 to 36 months, priced on the site's valuation at that point. All of that is spelled out before you sign anything.

What if I want to leave?

Call and ask before you sign, and get the answer in writing — from us or from anybody else you're considering. It's the single most important question people forget to ask, and any designer who gets vague about it is telling you something useful.

Do you do the SEO too, or just the design?

The structural side is part of every build — headings, schema, speed, crawler access, content written to answer real questions. Ongoing campaign work like link building, Google Business Profile management and content programs is handled through Eye To Ad Media, which is the same people.
