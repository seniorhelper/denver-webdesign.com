Common questions

Before you get in touch

How quickly will I hear back?

Within one business day, and usually sooner. If it's urgent, call rather than filling in the form — the phone is genuinely the faster route and you'll get a person rather than a queue.

Will I get a sales call afterwards?

No. One reply, from a person, answering what you asked. There's no automated sequence, no newsletter signup buried in the form and no follow-up cadence. If you don't reply, that's the end of it.

What should I have ready?

Very little. What your business does, roughly what a job is worth to you, and your current website address if you have one. If you've run PageSpeed Insights and have the mobile scores, even better — but it isn't required.

Do you charge for the initial conversation?

No. That includes telling you what's wrong with your current site, and you're welcome to take that answer to somebody else. We'd rather you got it right than got it from us.

Can we meet in person?

For most projects it isn't necessary and a phone call is faster for you. For larger projects in the Denver metro we can meet if it helps you feel confident about who you're hiring.

What if I'm not in Denver?

Not a problem. Most clients are in the metro, and we work across Colorado and out of state. The build is identical — local knowledge just matters more when your own customers search locally. More on that here.
