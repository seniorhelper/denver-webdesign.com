The honest ladder

Four things sold as “custom”

All four are legitimate products. Only one of them is genuinely custom, and the price difference between the top and bottom of this list is enormous.

01
Not custom

Template with your logo and colours

A purchased theme, your branding dropped in, demo content replaced. Published research puts pre-built templates at $0 to $200. Fast and cheap, and there is nothing wrong with it — as long as nobody charged you five figures for it.

02
Barely custom

Template assembled with a page builder

Sections dragged around inside Elementor or Divi until it looks different. This is what most "custom" quotes actually deliver. It works, and it ships a lot of weight to every visitor, and your content is now locked into that builder's format.

03
Partly custom

Custom design on a standard framework

A designer draws it properly, then it's built on an existing system. Genuinely bespoke visually, with sensible constraints underneath. Reasonable value and where a lot of good agency work lands.

04
Actually custom

Designed and coded from nothing

Structure decided by what your business needs, then written by hand. Nothing loads that you don't use. Published research puts custom design alone at $2,000 to $10,000, involving 20 to 40 hours of design work before a line of code exists. It's what we do, and it's the reason the sites are fast.

What to ask

How to tell which one you're being sold

You don't need to read code. Four questions separate genuinely custom work from a theme with a markup.

Good signs

Answers that mean custom

  • "We'll design the layout around your services first"
  • "No, we don't use a page builder"
  • "You'll see a real page, not a wireframe"
  • "Here's a site we built — run PageSpeed on it"
  • "The structure depends on what you actually sell"
  • "You can move it anywhere later"
Warning signs

Answers that mean template

  • "Pick a design you like from these examples"
  • "It's fully customisable" — that describes a theme
  • "We can add any feature with a plugin"
  • Vagueness about what it's actually built on
  • A portfolio where every site shares one layout
  • Their own site scores badly on PageSpeed

Both sides

When custom is worth it — and when it isn't

Worth it

Custom earns its cost when

  • Speed genuinely affects your business, and template weight is in the way
  • Your services don't fit the three-column layout everyone ships
  • You need something a plugin can't do — a calculator, a real booking flow
  • You're in a crowded market where looking identical costs you
  • You want to stop paying for maintenance you never chose
Skip it

A template is the right call when

  • You need something online this week for almost nothing
  • You're testing whether the business works at all
  • The site is a formality and customers come from elsewhere
  • Budget is genuinely the binding constraint right now
  • You'll replace it once you know what you actually need

Straight answers

Custom website questions

What does a custom website actually mean?

Properly, it means the design and structure were created for your business rather than adapted from an existing layout. In practice the word gets used for anything from a theme with your logo added to a site coded from nothing. Ask specifically what it's built on and whether a page builder is involved — the answers separate the two.

How much does a custom website cost?

Published 2026 research puts custom design work alone at $2,000 to $10,000 depending on page count and brand complexity, with a pre-built template at $0 to $200. Full custom builds from agencies commonly land between $8,000 and $22,000. Ours start at $99 a month with no setup fee.

Is a custom website better for SEO?

Not automatically, but it removes the main obstacles. Custom code means you control the heading structure, the schema, the URL structure and the page weight rather than working around what a theme decided. A well-built template site beats a badly built custom one — the advantage is in what's possible, not what's guaranteed.

Will my site look like everyone else's?

Not if it's genuinely custom. The reason so many local business sites feel familiar is that a handful of popular themes cover an enormous share of the web. If looking distinct matters in your market, that's one of the clearest arguments for building rather than buying a layout.

Can I edit a custom site myself?

Depends how it's built. Ours are static, so edits come to us and usually happen the same day at no extra cost. If editing it yourself is genuinely important, say so at the start and we'll build on something that supports it — usually WordPress, with the maintenance that comes with it.

How long does a custom website take?

Some single-page sites go live within 24 hours. A typical multi-page business site takes a week or more. The build is rarely the constraint — content and decisions are. We write the copy from a phone call so that doesn't stall on you.

What if I already have a template site?

Don't throw it out reflexively. If it ranks and converts, that history has value. We'd look at whether specific fixes solve the problem before recommending a rebuild. More on redesigns.

Do I own a custom website?

Your domain and your content are yours regardless. On our monthly plans the site itself is leased with a buyout option at 24 to 36 months, priced on its value then. Ask any supplier this question before signing, and get the answer in writing — it's the one people forget.

Is custom worth it for a small business?

It depends whether the website is meant to generate customers or just exist. If it's decoration, a template is fine and cheaper. If it's supposed to bring in work, the things custom gives you — speed, structure, distinctiveness — are the things that make that happen. More for small businesses.

Can a custom site be built cheaply?

Not as a one-off — the design hours are real. The monthly model works because the cost spreads out and we keep the relationship rather than disappearing after the invoice clears. That's how a genuinely hand-built site starts at $99 rather than $9,000.
